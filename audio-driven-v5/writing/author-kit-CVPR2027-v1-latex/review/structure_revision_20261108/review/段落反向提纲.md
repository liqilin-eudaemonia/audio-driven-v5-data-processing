## 02_related_work

- P01 **视觉倾听沿革**：Listening-generation methods synthesize facial expressions and head movements from a speaker's communication signals.
- P02 **行为适当性与事件表示**：Beyond visual quality, ReactFace~\cite{reactface} studies online generation of multiple appropriate facial reactions, considering appropriateness, diversity, and synchrony.
- P03 **已知音轨的交互视频**：Interactive-avatar methods also differ in whether the target audio is supplied or generated.
- P04 **自主AV回应与本文边界**：OmniResponse~\cite{omniresponse} is closer to this setting: it uses speaker facial information, transcripts, and dialogue history to generate aligned text and facial responses, with TempoVoice synthesizing audio for vocal responses.
- P05 **数据资源及本文组织**：Existing datasets cover different aspects of dyadic interaction.
- P06 **既有评价与本文协议**：Feedback evaluation has also moved beyond visual quality.

## 03_task

- P01 **输入输出**：Given a current speaker audio segment $a_s$ and the listener's first frame $I_l$, the model generates listener video $V_l$ and audio $a_l$ for the corresponding interval.
- P02 **倾听／发声边界**：We focus on brief feedback while the other participant holds the conversational floor.
- P03 **训练推理信息边界**：At inference, no dialogue history, future listener frames, target audio, or ground-truth reaction labels are provided.
- P04 **一对多属性及限制**：The current utterance does not uniquely determine all listener behavior.

## 04_data

- P01 **数据来源及目标**：We construct paired listening data from Seamless conversations~\cite{seamless}.
- P02 **切分与角色规则**：We use transcripts, word timings, speech activity, and acoustic pauses to determine local roles and candidate boundaries.
- P03 **媒体处理**：We screen and crop listener video and correct orientation in affected source clips.
- P04 **线索辅助描述**：Direct audiovisual descriptions were observed to omit some movements and brief vocal reactions.
- P05 **表情线索与时间证据限制**：These cues describe observable behavior: brow movements do not directly establish an internal emotional state.
- P06 **描述派生整段标签**：A separate, fixed behavior-extraction procedure uses Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507~\cite{qwen3} to extract visual and audio behavior names with supporting text from the descriptions.
- P07 **样本与规模**：Each sample links the current speaker audio, the listener's first frame, aligned listener video and audio, and a reaction description, together with source and temporal metadata.

## 05_method

- P01 **框架概览**：Our framework comprises a frozen Qwen2.5-Omni-3B model~\cite{qwen25omni}, a trainable connector, a Gemma text encoder~\cite{gemma_pending}, a speaker-audio Whisper branch~\cite{whisper}, and an LTX audiovisual generator~\cite{ltx_pending}.
- P02 **训练／推理描述来源**：During training, $d$ is the annotated description $d^*$ of the target listener media.
- P03 **Qwen版本与角色**：The current Qwen model is frozen and has not undergone planning SFT.
- P04 **直接语音分支**：High-level representations and text conditions may not fully expose local acoustic information to the generator.
- P05 **Feature设计**：General Qwen representations still need to be mapped to conditions suited to listener audiovisual generation.
- P06 **Feature参数与推理边界**：The losses train the auxiliary decoders and the shared connector to encode information useful for fitting and generating the target media.
- P07 **Event位置与聚合**：Continuous media-generation losses do not explicitly require feedback categories to appear.
- P08 **Event训练噪声步与非时序目标**：This supervision uses denoising hidden states at the sampled flow noise step; it does not require complete video sampling and decoding at each training step.
- P09 **Event标签和权重**：The targets follow the description-based extraction and conflict handling in \cref{sec:data}.
- P10 **Event类别与有效性**：Here $s_{ic}$ is a classification logit and $\alpha_c$ is a positive-class weight derived from training-label statistics.
- P11 **总体目标**：The native audiovisual objective $\mathcal L_{\mathrm{AV}}$ is the sum of video and audio flow-matching losses, computed on their valid generation supports.
- P12 **可训练参数与梯度**：We update the connector, feature decoders, event heads, LTX text-conditioning LoRA, Whisper adapter, and independent audiovisual cross-attention branches.
- P13 **实际AV训练集**：Generation training uses a separately prepared selection of 284,218 clips totaling approximately 500 hours.
- P14 **推理流程**：At inference, Qwen first predicts a reaction description from $a_s$ and $I_l$.

## 06_evaluation

- P01 **总体评价设计**：We extend an evaluation framework based on OmniResponse~\cite{omniresponse} with measures for listening feedback.
- P02 **一对多时序动机**：An utterance often constrains the affective or attitudinal direction of a response.
- P03 **类别一致性及局限**：We collapse repeated instances of a category within a clip into a presence label and compute precision, recall, and macro-F1 separately for visual and audio categories.
- P04 **视频指标定义**：Paired video evaluation includes PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, FID, FVD, Emo-Acc, Emo-Div, and $\Delta$Resp.
- P05 **视频指标解读边界**：These measures provide complementary observations, not complete judgments of interaction.
- P06 **声音活动定义及局限**：For brief, intermittent listener sounds, the implementation distinguishes energy-based acoustic activity from speech detected by a voice-activity detector.
- P07 **适用音质与同步**：For continuous speech windows that satisfy their input requirements, UTMOSv2 assesses audio quality and SyncNet's LSE-D and LSE-C assess synchronization between mouth motion and generated sound.
- P08 **语境与声画协调**：The design considers whether feedback fits the current speaker audio, is natural without being excessive, and conveys listening engagement.
- P09 **GLARE补充协议**：The framework also integrates GLARE's R-F1, R-tIoU, R-ATD, and R-FID~\cite{glare} for supplementary visual-reaction and temporal-alignment diagnostics.
