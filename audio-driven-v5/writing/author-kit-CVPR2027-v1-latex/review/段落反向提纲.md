## 3 Method

### 3.1 Task Definition and Overview

- **03_task/P01 · 任务——当前语音与首帧到同时间段的倾听视听反馈。**：Given the current speaker audio $a_s$ and the listener's first frame $I_l$, we generate listener video $V_l$ and audio $a_l$ over the corresponding interval.

- **03_task/P02 · 监督接口——先说明方法使用哪些数据，构建细节放第4章。**：Training uses paired listener audiovisual media, an ordered reaction description $d^*$, and clip-level behavior labels with validity masks.

- **05_method/P01 · 总览——理解条件、直接声学条件与联合生成器的分工。**：Our framework combines a description-conditioned representation of the intended response with direct acoustic information from the speaker (\cref{fig:method}).

- **05_method/P02 · Connector——层感知记忆与坐标查询将Qwen表征映射到两路条件。**：The connector normalizes and projects each selected Qwen layer into a shared memory, adding learned layer embeddings before concatenation.

- **05_method/P03 · 描述来源——训练用标注，推理预测并重新编码，描述是内部条件。**：During training, $d=d^*$ is the annotated description of the target response.

### 3.2 Direct Speaker-Audio Conditioning

- **05_method/P04 · Whisper动机与设计——保留直接声学信息，补充描述与高层条件。**：A reaction description summarizes intended behavior, but does not explicitly preserve every local acoustic cue in the speaker's utterance.

- **05_method/P05 · 时间与初始化——共同时间坐标、保留静音、无额外Whisper损失。**：Speaker keys and generation queries use temporal rotary positions defined on a common time origin in seconds.

### 3.3 Target Audiovisual Feature Supervision

- **05_method/P06 · Feature动机——让实际送入生成器的条件携带目标媒体信息。**：The connector must transform general multimodal representations into conditions useful for listener generation.

- **05_method/P07 · Feature公式——独立噪声下的特征流匹配，保留有效位置与安静音频。**：For each valid feature position, we sample Gaussian noise $\epsilon$ and a noise level $\sigma\sim\mathcal U(0,1)$, forming $x_{\sigma,m}=(1-\sigma)F_m+\sigma\epsilon$.

- **05_method/P08 · Feature梯度——监督Connector，冻结Qwen，推理无需教师或解码器。**：Because the decoders consume the same $U_m$ used by LTX, their gradients encourage the connector to retain information relevant to the target listener media.

### 3.4 Clip-Level Event Supervision

- **05_method/P09 · Event动机与读出——从生成表征而非条件U监督反馈类别存在。**：The continuous audiovisual objective does not explicitly specify which feedback categories should occur.

- **05_method/P10 · Event标签与公式——区分正例、弱负例和未知，按有效样本归一化。**：The targets are derived from reaction descriptions with the evidence checks described in \cref{sec:annotation}.

- **05_method/P11 · Event作用边界——整段存在性适配可变时序，但不保证所有生成行为正确。**：This objective supervises whether a category occurs within the clip, without imposing event start or end times.

## 4 Listening Data Construction

### 4.1 Role-Aware Pair Construction

- **04_data/P01 · 数据来源及目标**：We construct paired listening data from Seamless conversations~\cite{seamless}.

- **04_data/P02 · 切分与角色规则**：We use transcripts, word timings, speech activity, and acoustic pauses to determine local roles and candidate boundaries.

- **04_data/P03 · 媒体处理**：We screen and crop listener video and correct orientation in affected source clips.

### 4.2 Cue-Assisted Reaction Annotation

- **04_data/P04 · 线索辅助描述**：Direct audiovisual descriptions were observed to omit some movements and brief vocal reactions.

- **04_data/P05 · 表情线索与时间证据限制**：These cues describe observable behavior: brow movements do not directly establish an internal emotional state.

- **04_data/P06 · 描述派生整段标签**：A separate, fixed behavior-extraction procedure uses Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507~\cite{qwen3} to extract visual and audio behavior names with supporting text from the descriptions.

- **07_experiments/P01 · 实验证据链——整体能力、组件作用和资源／评价可信度。**：The experimental design addresses three questions: how the model performs on audiovisual backchannels and shared visual-listening capabilities, what each component contributes, and how reliably the data and evaluation capture listening behavior.

- **07_experiments/P03 · 已确认模型版本与训练实现**：Generation training uses frozen Qwen2.5-Omni-3B without planning SFT, with hidden states from layers 8, 18, 28, and 35.

### 5.2 Evaluation Protocol

- **06_evaluation/P01 · 总体评价设计**：We extend an evaluation framework based on OmniResponse~\cite{omniresponse} with measures for listening feedback.

- **06_evaluation/P02 · 一对多时序动机**：An utterance often constrains the affective or attitudinal direction of a response.

- **06_evaluation/P03 · 类别一致性及局限**：We collapse repeated instances of a category within a clip into a presence label and compute precision, recall, and macro-F1 separately for visual and audio categories.

- **06_evaluation/P04 · 视频指标定义**：Paired video evaluation includes PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, FID, FVD, Emo-Acc, Emo-Div, and $\Delta$Resp.

- **06_evaluation/P05 · 视频指标解读边界**：These measures provide complementary observations, not complete judgments of interaction.

- **06_evaluation/P06 · GLARE补充协议**：The framework also integrates GLARE's R-F1, R-tIoU, R-ATD, and R-FID~\cite{glare} for supplementary visual-reaction and temporal-alignment diagnostics.

- **06_evaluation/P07 · 声音活动定义及局限**：For brief, intermittent listener sounds, the implementation distinguishes energy-based acoustic activity from speech detected by a voice-activity detector.

- **06_evaluation/P08 · 适用音质与同步**：For continuous speech windows that satisfy their input requirements, UTMOSv2 assesses audio quality and SyncNet's LSE-D and LSE-C assess synchronization between mouth motion and generated sound.

- **06_evaluation/P09 · 语境与声画协调**：The design considers whether feedback fits the current speaker audio, is natural without being excessive, and conveys listening engagement.
