## 3 Task and Data Construction

### 3.1 Task Definition

- **03_task/P01 · 输入输出**：Given a current speaker audio segment $a_s$ and the listener's first frame $I_l$, the model generates listener video $V_l$ and audio $a_l$ for the corresponding interval.

- **03_task/P02 · 倾听／发声边界**：We focus on brief feedback while the other participant holds the conversational floor.

- **03_task/P03 · 训练推理信息边界**：At inference, no dialogue history, future listener frames, target audio, or ground-truth reaction labels are provided.

- **03_task/P04 · 一对多属性及限制**：The current utterance does not uniquely determine all listener behavior.

### 3.2 Paired Clip Construction

- **04_data/P01 · 数据来源及目标**：We construct paired listening data from Seamless conversations~\cite{seamless}.

- **04_data/P02 · 切分与角色规则**：We use transcripts, word timings, speech activity, and acoustic pauses to determine local roles and candidate boundaries.

- **04_data/P03 · 媒体处理**：We screen and crop listener video and correct orientation in affected source clips.

### 3.3 Cue-Assisted Reaction Annotation

- **04_data/P04 · 线索辅助描述**：Direct audiovisual descriptions were observed to omit some movements and brief vocal reactions.

- **04_data/P05 · 表情线索与时间证据限制**：These cues describe observable behavior: brow movements do not directly establish an internal emotional state.

- **04_data/P06 · 描述派生整段标签**：A separate, fixed behavior-extraction procedure uses Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507~\cite{qwen3} to extract visual and audio behavior names with supporting text from the descriptions.

### 3.4 Dataset Statistics

- **04_data/P07 · 样本与规模**：Each sample links the current speaker audio, the listener's first frame, aligned listener video and audio, and a reaction description, together with source and temporal metadata.

## 5 Experiments

- **07_experiments/P01 · 实验证据链——整体能力、组件作用和资源／评价可信度。**：The experimental design addresses three questions: how the model performs on audiovisual backchannels and shared visual-listening capabilities, what each component contributes, and how reliably the data and evaluation capture listening behavior.

### 5.1 Experimental Setup

- **07_experiments/P02 · 实际AV训练集与测试范围**：Generation training uses a separately selected set of 284,218 clips totaling approximately 500 hours.

- **07_experiments/P03 · 已确认模型版本与训练实现**：The conditioning model is frozen Qwen2.5-Omni-3B without planning SFT; its representations are taken from layers 8, 18, 28, and 35.

### 5.2 Evaluation Protocol

- **06_evaluation/P01 · 总体评价设计**：We extend an evaluation framework based on OmniResponse~\cite{omniresponse} with measures for listening feedback.

- **06_evaluation/P02 · 一对多时序动机**：An utterance often constrains the affective or attitudinal direction of a response.

- **06_evaluation/P03 · 类别一致性及局限**：We collapse repeated instances of a category within a clip into a presence label and compute precision, recall, and macro-F1 separately for visual and audio categories.

- **06_evaluation/P04 · 视频指标定义**：Paired video evaluation includes PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, FID, FVD, Emo-Acc, Emo-Div, and $\Delta$Resp.

- **06_evaluation/P05 · 视频指标解读边界**：These measures provide complementary observations, not complete judgments of interaction.

- **06_evaluation/P06 · GLARE补充协议**：The framework also integrates GLARE's R-F1, R-tIoU, R-ATD, and R-FID~\cite{glare} for supplementary visual-reaction and temporal-alignment diagnostics.

- **06_evaluation/P07 · 声音活动定义及局限**：For brief, intermittent listener sounds, the implementation distinguishes energy-based acoustic activity from speech detected by a voice-activity detector.

- **06_evaluation/P08 · 适用音质与同步**：For continuous speech windows that satisfy their input requirements, UTMOSv2 assesses audio quality and SyncNet's LSE-D and LSE-C assess synchronization between mouth motion and generated sound.

- **06_evaluation/P09 · 语境与声画协调**：The design considers whether feedback fits the current speaker audio, is natural without being excessive, and conveys listening engagement.
