On all 43 official ViCo test pairs, replacing the base Qwen planner with SFT2752 under an unchanged step-8000 generator increased the ratio of mean expression variance to GT from 37.2\% to 71.1\%. FID decreased from 30.116 to 29.426. Expression Delta-Frechet decreased from 0.03584 to 0.02591 (paired difference -0.00993, 95\% interval [-0.02747, -0.00330]). Framewise expression L1 increased from 0.15394 to 0.16719 (difference 0.01324, 95\% interval [0.00589, 0.03396]); PSNR, SSIM, CSIM and CPBD were also lower. The head-rotation L1 difference was -0.00951 with interval [-0.01827, 0.00500], which includes zero. These results show a trade-off between motion amplitude/dynamics and framewise correspondence. The generator was trained with base-Qwen hidden conditions; SFT replaces both predicted descriptions and hidden conditions at inference. Intervals resample source-video clusters and are conditional on fixed checkpoints and generation seed 137. Human-rated appropriateness was not measured.

Under the disclosed source-only transfer protocol, the SFT variant achieved expression/head-rotation L1 of 0.16719/0.08715, compared with 0.21209/0.10664 for DyStream and 0.22449/0.10487 for Avatar Forcing. The source-cluster intervals for all four paired L1 differences were below zero. Training budgets are unmatched; this is a common-input transfer comparison with the stated adaptations.
