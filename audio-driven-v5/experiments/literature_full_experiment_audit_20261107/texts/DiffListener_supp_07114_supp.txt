C. Additional Experiments

C.1. Ablation Study on Motion Conditions

To validate the effect of different generation conditions on
the motion generation module, We calculate Residual Pear-
son Correlation Coefficient (rPCC) between the listener’s
expressions (51-dim blend-shape) generated with different
conditions and the GT speaker’s expressions to assess the
motion synchronization, and Mean Squared Error (MSE)
to assess motion accuracy. As shown below, after adding
speaker’s audio and expression, the synchronization and ac-
curacy have both significantly improved.
