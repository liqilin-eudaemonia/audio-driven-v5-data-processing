A. Implementation Details

A.1. Network Details

We introduced Audio Encoder, Mapping Net, Motion En-
coder and Transformer Decoder-based Diffusion Model in
Section 3 of our main paper. Due to the page limitation,
we show implementation details of these modules in this
Appendix.

Audio Encoder  For audio preprocessing, we first extract
45-dim acoustic features from speaker audio, and then en-
code these acoustic features into audio features. The struc-
ture of Audio Encoder is shown in Figure 8, which consists
of two convolution layers, a GELU activation function, six
768-dim transformer layers, and a linear layer.

Mapping Net  As discussed in Section 3.2 of the main
paper, in order to obtain portrait-related tokens, we further
employ a Mapping Net to convert text embeddings from
RoBERTa [22] into static portrait tokens, which describes the
listener’s static portrait (e.g. expressions, head movements)
in the entire video segment. The structure of Mapping Net is
shown in Figure 8.

 The speaker says to the listener: "At that time, I'm full of disappointment in life...."
 Q: What is the speaker communicating to the listener? Given that the listener is an emotionally rich person who is very sympathetic to the speaker’s experiences, describe the listener's facial expressions and head
 movements in visual detail.
 A: The speaker is telling the listener that she felt deeply disappointed with life. The listener would likely appear sympathetic and listen with quite furrowed brow and without any special head movements.

 Example 2:
 The speaker says to the listener: "I don't think you did a good job on this matter..."
 Q: What is the speaker communicating to the listener? Given that the listener is a very impatient person and disagrees with the speaker’s words, describe the listener's facial expressions and head movements in visual
  detail.
 A: The speaker is telling the listener that he thinks the listener performed poorly on this matter. The listener would likely look angry and listen with a relatively lowered brow and shaking head.

  detail.
 A: The speaker is telling the listener that he thinks Lily did best among these people. The listener would likely look delighted and listen with relatively parted lip and lip corner raised completely and nodding head.
